# PancTrials

PancTrials is an iPhone app designed to help people find U.S. clinical trials that may be relevant to pancreatic cancer.

The app focuses on making trial discovery easier to understand by combining clinical-trial search with filters for location, biomarkers, sex, and keywords, while showing the registry evidence that caused a trial to appear.

## Features

- Search currently recruiting and soon-to-be-recruiting U.S. clinical trials
- Filter by U.S. state
- Filter by genetic marker or biomarker
- Filter by sex
- Add custom keywords such as `adenocarcinoma`, `metastatic`, or treatment-related terms
- Separate marker results into clear match categories
- Show why a trial matched, including relevant registry text
- Find potentially relevant solid-tumor or basket trials
- Save trials for later
- Highlight newly discovered trials
- View study locations, contacts, interventions, eligibility criteria, and registry links

## Important medical notice

PancTrials is an informational clinical-trial discovery tool.

It does **not** determine whether a person qualifies for a clinical trial, provide a diagnosis, or recommend treatment. Clinical-trial eligibility and enrollment must always be confirmed directly with the study team and appropriate medical professionals.

Trial status, locations, contacts, and eligibility criteria can change. Users should verify current information with the source registry and study team.

## Trial data

PancTrials uses public or official clinical-trial registry information. The exact source or sources used by the current app version are shown in the app where available.

PancTrials is not affiliated with or endorsed by ClinicalTrials.gov, the National Cancer Institute, the World Health Organization, or any individual clinical-trial sponsor unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Privacy

PancTrials does not require an account and is designed to keep app preferences and saved-trial information on the user's device.
