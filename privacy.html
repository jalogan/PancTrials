Privacy Policy for PancTrials

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PancTrials handles information when you use the app.

Information you provide to the app

PancTrials lets you enter or select search criteria such as U.S. states, genetic or biomarker terms, keywords, and eligibility-related filters. Some of these search terms may relate to health information.

How search information is used

PancTrials uses your search criteria only to retrieve and filter clinical-trial information. To perform live searches, the app may send the search parameters needed for the request to official clinical-trial registry services, including ClinicalTrials.gov and, when enabled in the app, other official registry services such as the National Cancer Institute (NCI) clinical-trial services or the World Health Organization ICTRP.

PancTrials does not operate a developer-controlled server that receives or stores your trial searches. External registry services may receive ordinary network information, such as your IP address, and may maintain server logs under their own privacy policies.

Information stored on your device

PancTrials may store app preferences and app state locally on your device, including selected filters, saved trial identifiers, and information used to identify trials that are new since a previous successful refresh. This information is used to provide app features and is not uploaded to a PancTrials account or developer-operated server.

Accounts, advertising, and tracking

PancTrials does not require you to create an account. PancTrials does not sell your personal information and does not use your information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Third-party clinical-trial services

Clinical-trial data and live search results are obtained from public or official registry services. Those services have their own privacy practices. Depending on the sources enabled in the app version, these may include:

  • ClinicalTrials.gov, operated by the U.S. National Library of Medicine.
  • National Cancer Institute (NCI) clinical-trial services.
  • World Health Organization ICTRP, if enabled as a live source.

The source or provenance of trial records is shown in the app where available.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored PancTrials preferences and saved-trial information remain on your device until you change or remove them, reset the relevant app state, or delete the app. Deleting PancTrials removes the app's locally stored data from that device, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.

PancTrials cannot delete logs or information that an external registry service may retain when servicing a network request. Such information is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

Medical information and eligibility

PancTrials is an informational discovery tool. It does not determine whether a person qualifies for a clinical trial, provide a diagnosis, or recommend treatment. Eligibility and enrollment must be confirmed with the clinical-trial study team and appropriate medical professionals.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if PancTrials' features, data sources, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
