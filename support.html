Please include, when useful:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version if the issue appears technical.
Please do not email medical records, genetic test reports, Social Security numbers, insurance information, or other sensitive personal or health information.

Clinical-trial questions

PancTrials helps users discover and review public clinical-trial information, but it does not determine eligibility or enroll participants. For questions about eligibility, enrollment, treatment, scheduling, travel, or study requirements, contact the study team listed in the trial record and discuss the trial with your medical team.

Trial data

PancTrials displays information obtained from official/public clinical-trial registry sources. Trial status, locations, contacts, and eligibility criteria can change. Always verify current information with the trial's source registry and study team before making decisions.
